Diplomacy rebuild — runtime244 — 17 November2026

Implemented
Diplomacy now opens on a parchment treaty palette and one political map. Select an overture, then select its recipient on the map or accessible country key. Alliances, trade consulates, embassies, non-aggression pacts, Old World minor empire invitations, peace and war use the existing campaign command/settlement path. Incoming diplomatic decisions and end-of-turn market restrictions are retained. Ineligible recipients are dimmed, and tapping one explains the reason.

Subsidies, grants, favoured partners and information are secondary modes. Selected minor nations display the actual favoured partner. Current trade terms appear in information/subsidy views. Pending offers have map scroll markers and rescind controls. An accepted input disarms the selected overture: later country taps inspect rather than sending more orders. Pending markers have a44CSS-pixel hit area, recalculated after layout/viewport changes. Phone layouts use an accessible short Info label.

The old Power15/Trade50 table is removed. Strategic strength remains internal to the existing AI logic. Separate presentation-only rankings show eight categories: army, navy, merchant marine, world exports, industry, labour, overseas profits and technology. Bars are relative within each category, rounded to5-percent display steps; no raw strength score is printed or exposed in the accessible label. Rankings are derived from current campaign data. Exact equivalence to every original game's ranking formula is not claimed.

Newly completed underlying diplomacy
Grants reserve money immediately, refund cancellation before delivery, survive save/reload and settle once at turn end. Locked grants renew when affordable and stop with news when they cannot be renewed. Amounts1000/3000/5000/10000 give2/4/6/10 relations, capped at255 with the native peacetime floor. Embassies are required in minors; Great Powers already have diplomatic contact.
Minor/tribal non-aggression pacts require an embassy, cost nothing and add10 relations when settled. A pact blocks direct attack; declaration removes it through the existing war penalty path. Pending offices can be rescinded for their paid cost.
