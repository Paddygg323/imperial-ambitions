Original Imperialism II reference playtest — in progress, 9 October 2026

This is a live UI playtest of an isolated unmodified original executable,
not a test of the rejected phone prototype. Campaign: England, historical
Europe, Normal (100). Started in 1502; reached 1506 so far.

Observed and performed:
- Selected the starting builder and ordered development of the hardwood
  forest south of London. Initially showed 0 Timber, Max Transport 0.
  In 1506, selecting that builder showed 1 Timber, Max Transport 1.
- Selected the engineer in London and ordered a road onto the wool tile
  immediately south of the capital, toward the timber tile.
- Recruited a second builder through Industry > Units > University.
  Cost displayed and consumed: $500 and 3 Paper. Arrived in 1504.
- Assigned Saw Mill, Sheep Ranching and Superior Hull Design using the
  A controls in the research list. The screen has three project slots,
  a separate goal slot, six category filters, per-project funding,
  estimates, detail and cancellation controls. Initial estimates were
  10, 12 and 10 turns at $100, $200 and $100 respectively.
- Production of one Lumber consumes two Timber and two Labour.
  Four starting labour points allowed two Lumber per turn.
- Selected the fleet in Port London; three Carracks were present.
  Sent one ship to Brittany Sea; its arrival was visible in 1504.
  Sent the same ship to Coral Ocean for the following turn.
- Turn resolution displayed the Deals screen before the turn summary.

Resumed 12 October 2026 from the England 1506 autosave. Reached 1518.
- Continued the carrack west through Rat Ocean, Clamshell Ocean,
  Orestian Ocean and Oleander Sea. Revealed the New World coastline.
- Deployed the Explorer onto that coast in 1514. In 1516, surrounding
  inland terrain became visible. Moved onto a hill; in 1518 the town
  of Jauja and an unexplored mountain became visible. Ordered prospecting.
- Saw Mill completion was reported on the 1514 summary (1512 orders).
  Superior Hull Design completion was reported in 1518 (1516 orders).
  These are observations, not fixed research-duration guarantees.
- Selected all three London regiments: one Pikemen, one Arquebusiers,
  one Knights. Moved through Yorkshire to Northumberland.
- Declared war on Scotland via Diplomacy > Diplomatic Overtures > War.
  Declaration took effect on resolution; attack orders were unavailable
  before war was active.
- Assaulted Ayr after the insufficient-cannon-strength warning.
  Entered actual tactical deployment and manually moved the Pikemen.
  Pikemen display: FP 0, Melee 5, Range 1, Defence 5, Speed 3.
  Arquebusiers: FP 5, Melee 1, Range 3, Defence 3, Speed 2.
  Enabled tactical autoplay using its lock control. AI withdrew;
  attack failed at battle turn 4, with no casualties on either side.
  Report listed two Scottish Bowmen. Scotland kept Ayr.
- Queued two additional workers: each costs two Fabric. Labour and
  unit recruitment explicitly do not support computer control.
- Unlocked Fluyte construction: one worker, six Lumber, two Cast Iron,
  two Fabric; cargo 4, speed 2, range 4, firepower 3, armour 95, hull 35.
  Construction has not yet been completed at this checkpoint.
- Production and trade were delegated to their original AI controls.
  No successful international sale/purchase has yet been observed.

Further checkpoint: 1528.
- Sheep Ranching completed in 1520. Queued a Fluyte and a Culverin in
  1520; both arrived in 1522. Construction consumed workers from the
  labour pool. Reducing paper production released two labour points
  needed for recruitment. Culverin cost: 1 worker, 1 Fabric, 4 Bronze.
- Reinforced Northumberland with the Culverin and assaulted Ayr again
  in 1524. The insufficient-cannon warning did not appear this time,
  but the automatically resolved assault still failed.
- Explorer discovered Tobacco (1526), Sugar Cane and Spices (1528),
  while revealing further inland terrain and mineral icons.
- Delegated research selected Horse Artillery as its goal and funded
  Animal Husbandry, Mine Engineering and Diplomatic Expertise.
- Manually bid for Wool, Fabric and Bronze in 1526. Accepted 1 Bronze
  from Portugal and 1 from Holland at $244 each. The Deals screen
  confirmed both purchases and a sale of 1 Lumber to Denmark for $279.
  Net trade balance was -$209. No Wool/Fabric offers arrived that turn.

Checkpoint: 1560, 12 October.
- War with France was declared in 1528 and active in 1530.
- Sailed 1 Fluyte and 1 Carrack, leaving 1 Carrack in Port London.
  Moving the last home ships triggers a warning that trade and sea
  transport would stop. Fluyte movement range limited the return voyage.
- Established a Brittany beachhead; its cannon marker was visibly
  attached to the fleet. Troops had to be in London (a port province).
- Ordered 4 regiments to land in Brittany in 1536, spending $400.
  Landing resolved into a battle against 1 Pikemen, 1 Arquebusiers and
  1 Knights. Attack failed; France kept Brittany.
- Accepted France's later peace offer; treaty confirmed in 1550.
  Returned the two invasion ships to Port London via Brabant Narrows.
- Civilian Book confirmed one Builder was Building Wool to level 2
  in Kent. Another Builder had merely redeployed to a livestock tile.
  A redeployment alone must not be reported as a completed upgrade.
- Mine Engineering completed in 1538, Diplomatic Expertise in 1544,
  Animal Husbandry in 1546. Cotton discovery was reported in 1554.
- At 1560, research screen showed Printing Press: $200, 4 turns;
  Copper and Tin Mining: $0, 20 turns; Organized Regiments: $0, 28 turns.
  Zero funding can leave a project reserved without advancing it.
  Cotton, Sugar and Tobacco Planting were present in available research.
- From 1538 onward, routine turns were advanced with production,
  transport, trade and research under their original AI controls.
  This portion is observed progression, not hands-on verification of
  every AI decision. The full game has not ended.

The full campaign is NOT complete. This checkpoint does not verify
colonial development, a successful siege, later eras, or the victory
screen. Do not treat it as proof of full original-game fidelity or a
completed phone build.
