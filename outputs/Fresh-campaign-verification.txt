Fresh normal campaign verification - 2026-11-10, build3-116

Scope
A fresh createGame() campaign, no starting boosts and no scripted wars. England
used the economy planner and declined incoming purchases. It issued no military
orders. Tactical AI drove battles on both sides. This is an integration test,
not competent human play, competitive balance proof, or Pixel testing.

Verified result
Turn155 /1755: Sweden captured London (province e1-0). The engine persisted
status=defeat, reason=capitalLost, nation=england, attacker=sweden. England was
marked not alive; remaining5 provinces became anarchic. No ranking-based claim.
Sweden had invaded Scotland, declared on England at151, and taken three English
provinces by155. The actual terminal save reloads through the packaged browser;
heading says Defeat, reports explain capital loss, End Turn is disabled after
reload. Screenshot inspected at412x915: phone-campaign-defeat.png.

Why England lost
The passive driver produced47 fit workers but no defending regiments. Sweden
had17 regiments,17 provinces and sufficient naval capacity to land its army.
This deliberately limited driver cannot establish the AI's strength against a
human. It does establish that the native-cost economy, research, recruitment,
naval staging, declarations, land combat, conquest and terminal state can connect
in one campaign beginning from the default initial state.

Failures exposed
France and Holland repeatedly failed against Switzerland without adapting.
Holland fell to6 workers while retaining26 regiments. Spain's treasury ended
at-80. All recorded starvation totals were0, but that does not prove robust
wartime economic management. Opponent assessment, restraint after failed sieges,
peace/alliance behavior and naval escort decisions need further work.
