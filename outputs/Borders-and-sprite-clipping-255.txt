Province borders and sprite clipping — 19 November 2026

Changes
- Province boundaries use pale ink with a dark keyline and remain visible in
  local, nation and world views. National frontiers remain wider and carry each
  country's colour on its own side.
- Borders are only drawn between two surveyed land tiles in different
  provinces. Coastlines, same-province tile edges and hidden provinces are not
  added to the political outlines.
- Hardwood/scrub forest, horses and grain levels 0–4 use a narrow perimeter mask
  with a 10% inset. This preserves canopies, the horse enclosure and field edges
  previously erased by the broad terrain/grass feather. Their outer margin still
  exposes the same continuous grass as neighbouring tiles.
- All source PNGs, library artwork, map coordinates, resources and gameplay
  systems are unchanged. The new border module is included in the offline cache.
