Imperial Ambitions 0.2.63 — Unslop visual audit and implementation
24 November 2026

DIRECTION
Period strategy, legible at action scale. Navy enamel holds instruments; warm
parchment holds information; brass frames the page; painted subjects carry the
identity. Preserve Paddy's chosen resource/terrain artwork and game systems.

AUDITED
Local/nation/world map, civilian cabinet, industry/workers/recruitment, transport,
research, trade/order resolution, diplomacy/treaties/grants/information, armies,
fleets, news and land battles. 60 baseline and final menu/view captures at
1448x1080, 412x915, 915x412 and 360x800. Runtime sprite mappings and major terrain
families checked; this does not claim every animation frame was repainted.

IMPLEMENTED
- Shared presentation layer for spacing, controls, readable type, focus and
  selected states, quieter vellum, enamel and hierarchy. Gold reserved for
  principal actions rather than every navigation tab.
- Commodity images in trade cards and actual offers; clearly grouped buy/sell
  controls, bid count, stock, prices and a browsable catalogue. Visible scroll
  treatment. Order handlers and trade engine unchanged.
- Correct source-atlas clipping: no neighbouring image fragments in stockpile
  portraits. Matching mineral icons instead of tin borrowing the copper image.
- Compact research vacancies, topic-specific illustrations, clearer funding and
  catalogue layout. Existing goals, prerequisites and funding unchanged.
- Illustrated grant denominations and a compact desktop header; treaty-first
  map, exact relation-marker positions and grant modes preserved.
- Larger army/fleet portraits, explicit selection, consistent rosters and
  content-sized desktop navy window.
- Calmer sea detail by zoom and a continuous land-colour wash beneath roads,
  towns and units. Existing chosen sprites and ownership borders preserved.
- Battle cover uses painted assets, local formations are larger, forts have
  masonry/connected segments and breach gaps. Quiet overview without overlapping
  labels; landscape height and roster/detail layout repaired. No terrain or
  combat rules invented. Full battle screenshot is a synthetic visual fixture
  using real battle objects and deployment, not a claim about campaign balance.
- Phone minimap moved below the control row so it cannot intercept Map Only.

VERIFIED
- All 967 gameplay/unit tests pass; 51 runtime sprite mappings and 121 library
  entries validate. Five publication/server-boundary checks pass.
- Menu audit: no browser errors, missing files or document/modal overflow across
  60 captures. Browsing menus and map views leaves the save byte-identical.
- Research categories/search/goals, base funding, increases/decreases, reload,
  project slots and desktop/phone layouts pass.
- Industry/recruitment imagery, automatic-labour preference, resource-specific
  production controls and real remote-port cargo allocation/reload pass.
- Illustrated trade buy/cancel, sell amount and saved bid reload pass; acceptance
  of a real minor-country timber offer is recorded by the trade engine.
- Fleet naming/reload and selected-only aggression pass. An old test used an
  archival Android copy and assumed every ship was selected; it now tests the
  canonical source and verifies that unselected ships are unchanged.
- Diplomacy treaty/map orders, cancellation/refunds, grants/recurring toggle,
  subsidies, exact relation positions, exports and keyboard selection pass.
- Minimap pan, map-only/restore and phone/landscape layouts pass without changing
  the saved campaign.
- Tactical deployment/undeployment, movement, overview/local zoom, AI handover,
  firing, bombardment, wall damage/breaches, retreat and cleanup pass. Actual
  campaign battle deployment/save/reload/result/return also passes.
- Runtime export and publication-boundary checks pass; isolated desktop/phone
  startup decodes all 51 sprite mappings without errors.

REVIEW AND LIMITS
Claude reviewed actual before/after screenshots twice: 6.5/10 then 7/10. It found
the pass a material improvement and identified flat paper, sparse battle art,
navy whitespace and weak trade scroll cues. Paper texture, battlefield cover,
forts/overview, navy sizing and scroll cues were addressed. Research remains
plainer than the other screens and deserves further owner/designer review.

No Pixel was used this turn and no Android package was installed. This does not
complete campaign validation, new civilian animations, battle effects, store
requirements or every backlog item. Existing browser campaigns were not opened
or overwritten; all interactive test campaigns used isolated browser storage.
