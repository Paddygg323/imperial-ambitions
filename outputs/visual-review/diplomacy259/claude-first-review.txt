Three concrete issues remain:

**1. Mobile nation-chip row clips the last country instead of wrapping (high impact)**
In both `treaties-412.png` and `information-412.png`, the "NATIONS · SELECT ON THE MAP OR BELOW" strip shows France, England, Sweden fully, then a sliver of a fourth shield (Italy) cut off against the right-hand gold frame — it's unreadable and untappable. Cause: `.diplomatic-desk .atlas-key` (`menu-theme.css:67`) is `display:flex` with no `flex-wrap`, and the mobile media query (`menu-theme.css:96-132`) never adds one, so items that can't shrink below their min-content width overflow past the panel edge and get visually eaten by the decorative frame. Since this is supposed to be the easy tap-target alternative to the small map blobs, losing an entry defeats its purpose — and it'll get worse with more than 4 known nations.
Fix: add `flex-wrap:wrap` (plus a little `row-gap`) to `.atlas-key` inside the `max-width:700px` block so chips wrap to a second row instead of spilling off-panel.

**2. Duplicate shield/emblem icon in the dossier header for nations without a portrait (medium)**
For Italy (no entry in `envoyPortrait`'s 6-nation list), the dossier shows a generic circular `envoy-seal` containing Italy's emblem, and then immediately repeats the same emblem inline before the name in the `<h4>` — visible as two shields back-to-back in both `treaties-desktop.png` and `information-412.png` ("🛡 Italy" right after the seal/portrait block). Source: `diplomacy-screen.mjs:71` calls `nationEmblem(target,…)` twice — once in the `envoy-seal` fallback, once in the `<h4>`. For the six majors this reads fine (illustrated portrait + small flag), but every minor nation (the majority of the roster) gets two near-identical shields stacked, which looks redundant/cluttered rather than deliberate.
Fix: when falling back to `envoy-seal` (no portrait), drop the inline `nationEmblem` from the `<h4>` since the seal already carries it — only portraits need the extra flag icon to disambiguate.
